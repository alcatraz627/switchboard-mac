# Switchboard, gold star: one way to drive every surface

> Owner, 2026-10-03: "I was taking about the mac os switchboard btw on finishing all and making things coherent and comprehensive for power users. The hub is good as is ... but I want the switchboard to be the gold star." Earlier the same day: collapse and expand for pinned panels (Notes, Usage), several panels in one window as vertical cards, and the slider wheel audit.

Evidence: `.claude/output/20261003-sb-goldstar/inventory.md` (every tab, hover page and pinned window, with file:line) and its 26 renders in `shots/`. Repo at the hub-nine commit.

## What the model missed

1. **Interaction was built per surface, not per component.** Keys exist on three tabs (Notes, Home, Timers) because each got its own router (`Keys.swift` `KeyRouter.handle`). The other 13 tabs are made of two shared rows (`SystemRowView`, `PolicyRowView`), and those rows have no keyboard at all. One row type with keys would have covered all 13.
2. **Wheel targets are named by what a person reads, not by what they are.** `Usage.swift:411` keys a slider by its label, so Claude's and Codex's "Warn at" share one target and move together. The policy slider (`PolicyPanel.swift:1289`) and the bulb hue slider (`Lights.swift:488`) register no target, so the wheel does nothing on them.
3. **A pinned surface is a lesser copy of the panel.** A pinned tab gets the tab keys but not the panel's; a pinned page gets no keys; nothing can be collapsed; panels cannot share a window; a pinned page stays 320 wide in a resizable window (`DeskPanels.swift`, inventory "Desk panels").
4. **The same input looks different in five places** (inventory "Cross-cutting facts": `SearchField` hand-rolls the field look, timer rename and note tags use bare fields).

## The model, as a coherence expectation

- Every row that does something can be reached with ↑/↓, acted on with Return (or →), opened and closed with →/←, switched with Space, and stepped with ←/→ when it is a slider. Escape steps back out. This holds on every tab, in the panel and when pinned.
- Every slider answers the wheel, one target per slider, up raises.
- A pinned panel is the panel: same keys, collapsible to its title row, and able to share a window with others as stacked cards.
- One field look everywhere (`inputBox`).
